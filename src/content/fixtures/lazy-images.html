Patterns that used to lose their images

Lazy loaders park a grey placeholder in the src attribute and keep the real image URL somewhere else entirely, which is a thing this reader has to cope with.

A second common shape puts an inline SVG placeholder in the src, so the real candidates live in a data-srcset list that has to be parsed properly.

Image CDNs encode their transforms as comma-separated parameters right in the path, so a naive srcset split turns a good URL into a broken one.

Notebooks and plotting libraries export their charts as base64 PNGs embedded directly in the document, and those are real content worth keeping around.
