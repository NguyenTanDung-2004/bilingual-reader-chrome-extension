How I Cut My Deploy Time in Half

Alex Chen · 6 min read · Feb 3, 2026
Our CI pipeline used to take 22 minutes per deploy. That is a long time to wait, e.g. when you are trying to ship a one-line hotfix at 5 p.m. on a Friday.

The bottleneck, it turned out, was not the build itself but the test suite. We were running the full suite, roughly 4.5 thousand tests, on every single push regardless of what changed.

Here is the core change, expressed as a small config diff:

jobs:
  test:
    strategy:
      matrix:
        shard: [1, 2, 3, 4]
    steps:
      - run: npm run test -- --shard=${{ matrix.shard }}/4

Splitting the suite across four shards, run in parallel, brought the test stage from 14 minutes down to about 4. Combined with a warmer build cache, the whole pipeline now finishes in under 10 minutes.

"Don't optimize the part that feels slow. Measure first." That was the one lesson that mattered most here.

What we tried that didn't work

We first tried, e.g., simply throwing more CPU at a single runner. It helped a little, about 12%, but nowhere near the 55% improvement sharding gave us.

  • Bigger single runner: modest gain, higher cost.
  • Skipping tests on docs-only changes: risky, and saved little time in practice.
  • Sharding the test suite: the actual win.

If your pipeline is slow, profile it before you touch anything. Ours turned out to be a parallelism problem, not a hardware one.
